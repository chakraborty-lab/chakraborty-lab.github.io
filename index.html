Research Across Scales

Connecting microscopic physics to engineering systems

We bridge fundamental physics and predictive computation to understand multiscale fluid, material, and propulsion phenomena and enable better aerospace and energy systems.

Atomistic

Molecular dynamics, reactive systems, interfacial physics and thermodynamics.

Mesoscale

Coarse graining, microstructure, particle systems and multiphase phenomena.

Continuum

Transport, fluid mechanics, phase change and reduced-order models.

Aerospace Systems

Propulsion, extreme environments, advanced fluids and engineering applications.

Research

What we study

We combine computational physics, molecular simulation and artificial intelligence to study complex engineering systems.

01

Molecular & Multiscale Modeling

Molecular dynamics, coarse-grained modeling, phase behavior, near-critical fluids, interfaces and transport phenomena.

02

Propulsion & Energy

Fuel behavior, high-pressure fluids, phase change, advanced propulsion concepts, energetic systems and thermal-fluid processes.

03

Extreme Environments & Materials

Gas-surface interactions, very-low-Earth-orbit environments, reactive materials and physics under extreme conditions.

04

AI for Physical Modeling

Machine learning, scientific AI, surrogate models, coarse graining, and data-driven discovery for complex physical systems.
